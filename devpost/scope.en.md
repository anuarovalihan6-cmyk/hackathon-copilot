# Hackathon Copilot

English translation of the [approved Russian scope](scope.md). This translation does not change the agreed scope.

One preparation page for one hackathon: its deadline, important updates, team tasks, and progress in one place.

## The Unique Kernel

Preparation connects an important hackathon update to the team's next work: a participant saves an update, adds the necessary task, assigns a person, and sees progress toward readiness. In the first version, the person managing the page makes these decisions manually.

## Who It's For

A hackathon participant organizing their team's preparation in one place on their computer. The first user is the beginner participant creating this project.

## The Core Loop

Open the hackathon page, check the deadline, important updates, and remaining tasks. Manually add updates with source links and tasks, assign people, and mark completed work. Return to the saved state and continue preparation with visible progress.

## Inspiration & Identity

A small, understandable preparation helper. During `3-prd`, the user chose a dark background, bright accents, and smooth animations. The specific direction and references are recorded in the PRD's **Look and Feel** section.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

This is the user's first independent experience building an app with AI and participating in a hackathon. The goal is a useful working result, reached through small steps with understandable explanations.

## What "Working" Looks Like

Set the hackathon's name and deadline, save an important update with a source link, add a task, and optionally assign a participant on one page. Completing work changes visible preparation progress. After closing and reopening at the same address in the same browser profile, records and completion marks remain. The interface switches between English and Russian and remembers the choice while preserving user-entered text and preparation data. This workflow can be shown in a short demo.

## The POC Boundary

- One preparation page for one hackathon, managed by one person on their computer.
- Hackathon name and deadline.
- Important updates with source links, entered manually.
- Tasks with optional assignees and completion marks; a person chooses assignments.
- Visible task completion progress.
- Browser persistence between visits.
- EN/RU interface switching with a remembered choice, explicitly requested during `3-prd`.
- Optional six-task starter plan: idea, MVP, testing, GitHub, video, and submission. A next-step card identifies the first unfinished task and offers a hint; the deadline shows remaining calendar days. This addition was accepted during final review.

## Later

Automatic news collection, online collaborative editing, and automatic task assignment require additional decisions and a larger scope. They are deferred until after the small working version.

## Explicitly Cut

Paid tools and services, following the user's explicit preference.
